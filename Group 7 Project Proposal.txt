2. Brief Description


Description: 
Our project’s application is done through a donation system such as donating clothes, food, or money to the people in need. and the excess resources are redistributed to a community pantry or shelter to help aid community members in transition. Our program strives to help empower mutual aid by enabling people to make donations of things like clothes, food, and daily essentials to local community shelters. Users are able to access the program anytime and anywhere, thus making donations easier and more efficient. Additionally, our program is completely optional and provides calculations of how much they want to donate to, donations for instance direct-to-individual giving or to do facility-based distribution.


3. Problem Statement:


Problem Identified: 
Our project aims to address the lack of access to basic everyday necessities, such as food, clothing, and daily essentials, among individuals and families in need. Many communities experience poverty or difficulties in obtaining these resources. However, the lack of an accessible and organized donation system can make it difficult to connect donors with those who need assistance. This is why we are proposing Aidfinity, to make donating easier, more accessible, and more efficient by providing a platform where people can contribute resources according to their willingness and capacity. Donations can be distributed directly to individuals or households, or through community pantries and shelters. In addition, excess resources can be redistributed to other organizations or communities that need them. Through this system, our project hopes to encourage mutual aid, reduce resource waste, and strengthen community support by making essential resources more accessible to everyone.


Target Users / Beneficiaries:
* Our target users/beneficiaries are individuals with limited resources and community members in need.

6. Sample Wireframe of the Application


Wireframe / Flowchart Area:
  









Screen Description:
* Log a donation screen: This screen allows users to record their donations by entering the necessary information, such as the donor’s name, donation category, item description, and quantity or monetary amount.
* Calculate donations screen: This screen displays the calculated totals of donations received.
* Select distribution method screen: This screen allows users to select how their donations will be distributed.
* Track and view donations screen: This screen allows users to view previously recorded donations and monitor their distribution progress.
